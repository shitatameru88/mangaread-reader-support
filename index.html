MangaRead Reader Support

MangaRead Reader is a private, offline-first manga and comic reader for your own CBZ and ZIP files.

Email Support Privacy Policy

Importing files

Use the import action in the app to choose CBZ or ZIP comic archives from the Files app. Imported library data stays on your device.

No bundled content

MangaRead Reader does not include or preload manga or comic content. Users are responsible for importing content they have the right to use.

Privacy-first

The app does not require an account. Reading progress, downloads, settings, and library data are stored locally unless you export them.
